Boróo

Boróo (Draft)

Boróo is an Ngowan language, it is highly analytic, with SVO word order and CV syllable structure.

Phonology

The sound inventory of Boróo is very hard to put in a nice table pretty small.

Labial Alveolar Dorsal Glottal
Voiceless t k ʔ
Voiced b d g
Continuant m r w ʍ h
  • /w/ is preglottalized on onsets
  • /m/ is labialized at codas
iua

It has a high and low tone.

Orthography

The sounds /b t d k g r h/ are written with the same letters as the IPA, these others are different:

/w/ /ʍ/ /h/ /i/ /u/ /a/
u hu h e o a

/m/ is ⟨m⟩ but ⟨um⟩ on codas to reflect its pronunciation as [mʷ]. The glottal stop /ʔ/ is not written in the orthography but it is present on the onset of syllables starting with a vowel.
