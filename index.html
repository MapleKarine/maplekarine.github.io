Conlangs

Evea

The worldbuilding of Evea is divided in different time periods, all of them span the entirety of bronze age tho. Some interesting things: Eleli is my first conlang, all the others were made just to help it, but it got out of control; Mutesz developed the first writing system, all other languages have scripts descending from it.

  • Mutesz
  • Zíg
  • Kalada languages
  • Kathłat
  • Binyekä
  • Eleli
  • Ialin
  • Kvrit
